## Interpretation

B1 is the **realistic floor**. TF-IDF + LogReg captures the lexical patterns of pathology language without semantic understanding. PubMedBERT (E1) must clear B1 by a substantial margin on the early-stop metric (>0.10 absolute) to justify its compute cost.

Per-axis expectations: lexical axes (topography, morphology, stem) should see large lifts over B0 — TF-IDF can match `'left breast'` or `'invasive ductal'`. Contextual axes (grade, behavior) and rare multi-label codes should remain low — these need semantic understanding the bag-of-ngrams cannot provide.
